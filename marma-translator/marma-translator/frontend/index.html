📖 User Guide

1️⃣

Select Languages

Choose your source and target languages from the dropdown menus. We support Bangla, English, and Marma in different scripts.

2️⃣

Enter Text

Type or paste your text in the source text area. The system accepts text of various lengths for translation.

3️⃣

Translate

Click the "Translate" button and wait for the AI model to process your text. Results appear in seconds.

4️⃣

Review Results

Check the translation in the target text area. The system uses state-of-the-art NMT models for accurate results.

5️⃣

Copy & Use

Use the "Copy Translation" button to copy the result to your clipboard for use in your work.

6️⃣

Provide Feedback

Help us improve! Click the "Feedback" button to share your experience and suggestions.

💡 Pro Tips

  • ✓ For best results, use complete sentences rather than single words.
  • ✓ The system works best with formal and academic language.
  • ✓ Use the swap button (⇄) to quickly reverse translation direction.
  • ✓ Always verify critical translations with native speakers.
